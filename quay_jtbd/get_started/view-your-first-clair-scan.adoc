= View your first Clair scan
:context: view_your_first_clair_scan

[role="_abstract"]
To know security scanning works in your registry, you can open the Clair vulnerability report in the UI. Quay.io and Operator-managed registries typically include Clair by default; standalone proof-of-concept and high availability deployments require Clair to be deployed first.
