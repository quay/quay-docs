= Verifying that the registry upgrade completed

[role="_abstract"]
To confirm that a {productname} registry advanced after a {producty} Operator upgrade that involves the `cache` component, you can check the registry version, availability, and rollout block conditions. Do not rely on availability alone.

.Prerequisites

* You completed one of the following procedures:
** xref:keep-customized-cache-configuration-after-50-crd[Keeping your customized cache configuration after the {producty} CRD is installed]
** xref:use-operator-managed-cache-defaults-before-upgrade[Using Operator-managed cache defaults before upgrading]
** xref:recover-blocked-registry-after-50-upgrade[Recovering a registry that is already blocked after the {producty} Operator upgrade]

.Procedure

. Confirm that the registry advanced to the Operator version, that the registry remains available, and that the rollout block is cleared:
+
[source,terminal]
----
$ oc get quayregistry <example_registry> -n <namespace> -o jsonpath='{.status.currentVersion}{"\n"}{.status.conditions[?(@.type=="Available")]}{"\n"}{.status.conditions[?(@.type=="RolloutBlocked")]}{"\n"}'
----
+
`status.currentVersion` must match the {producty} Operator version. `RolloutBlocked` must not remain `True` with reason `ConfigInvalid`. Do not rely on availability alone, because a blocked registry can still report that the previous deployment is available.
